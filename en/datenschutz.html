Privacy Policy

1. Privacy at a glance

General information

The following notes provide a simple overview of what happens to your personal data when you use the landing page (postureshift.info) or the app (app.postureshift.info). Personal data is any data that can be used to personally identify you. For detailed information, please refer to the privacy policy set out below this text.

Data collection when using PostureShift

Who is responsible for data collection?

Data processing is carried out by the operator of the app. You can find their contact details in the "Notice regarding the responsible party" section of this privacy policy.

How do we collect your data?

Some of your data is collected because you provide it to us, e.g. by voluntarily creating a user account (email address) to sync your posture rotations across devices. Other data is collected automatically by our IT systems, or by the hosting provider we use, when you visit the pages (e.g. technical data such as browser type or the time of access).

What do we use your data for?

Some of the data is collected to ensure the page and app are provided without errors. If you create a user account, data is also used to sync your rotations, settings, and custom postures across devices.

What rights do you have regarding your data?

You have the right at any time to receive free information about the origin, recipients, and purpose of your stored personal data. You also have the right to request that this data be corrected or deleted. If you have given consent to data processing, you can revoke this consent at any time for the future. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority.

2. Hosting and infrastructure

Hosting

We host the landing page and the app with All-Inkl. The provider is ALL-INKL.COM - Neue Medien Münnich, Inh. René Münnich, Hauptstraße 68, 02742 Friedersdorf (hereinafter: All-Inkl). Details can be found in All-Inkl's privacy policy: https://all-inkl.com/datenschutzinformationen/.

The use of All-Inkl is based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. We have a legitimate interest in the most reliable possible presentation of our page and app.

Data processing agreement

We have entered into a data processing agreement (DPA) with the provider named above. This is a contract required by data protection law that ensures the provider processes our visitors' personal data only on our instructions and in compliance with the GDPR.

Our own backend (user account, synchronisation)

For the optional user account and cross-device synchronisation of your rotations, we operate our own, self-hosted database (MySQL/MariaDB) with the hosting provider named above — no third-party service is used for accounts or data storage. To send the login link by email, we use the SMTP service of the same hosting provider to send an email to the address you provided.

4. User account and synchronisation (app.postureshift.info)

If you choose to create a user account in order to sync your rotations and settings across devices, we collect your email address (for passwordless login via magic link) as well as the rotations you create (selected postures and times per tab, "Office"/"Car"), your settings (e.g. intervals, notification channels), and any custom postures you add yourself. This data is required to provide you with the synchronisation feature (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

During the login process, a one-time login link, valid for 15 minutes, is sent by email; the associated security token is stored on our side only in hashed form. Your session is managed via a cookie that is strictly technically necessary (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR and Section 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG) and does not require separate consent.

Without a user account, you can continue to use the app entirely locally on your device; in this case, no data is transmitted to us.

5. Cookies and local storage

The app uses technically necessary local storage for your rotations, settings, and timer status, so that the app also works fully offline. This storage takes place exclusively on your device (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR and Section 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG) and does not require separate consent.

If you use a user account, a technically necessary session cookie is additionally set (see Section 4).

6. Server log files

Our hosting provider automatically collects and stores information in server log files that your browser automatically transmits to us: browser type and version, operating system used, referrer URL, time of the server request, and IP address. This data is not merged with data from other sources. Collection is based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in the technically error-free provision of our service).

7. Notifications (sound, voice output, push)

If you wish, the app can use audible cues (Web Audio API), voice output (the browser's built-in Web Speech API), and browser push notifications. These features run entirely locally in your browser or via its built-in interfaces; no data is transmitted to us or to third parties in the process. For push notifications, your browser will ask you for separate permission, which you can revoke at any time in your browser settings.
